She carries a famous name — and a more famous question: *what does a queen do when the trade route does the colonizing now?*

Shea Sisters is her answer. She has spent two years building the legal and logistics scaffolding for a women's cooperative that exports directly to European and West African buyers, cutting out the middlemen who quietly take 60 cents on the dollar.
