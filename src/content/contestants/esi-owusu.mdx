A working artist before she was a contestant — Esi runs a Friday afternoon class out of her parents' garage that has, over four years, grown into something the city now expects.

She wants the crown to amplify it. The platform fee from this cycle, if she wins, becomes the deposit on a proper space — and the photograph of her in the sash becomes the recruitment poster.
