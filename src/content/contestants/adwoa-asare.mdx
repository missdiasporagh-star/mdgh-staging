She was a marine-biology student before she was anything else — and her thesis was on how the inhabitants of one fishing village in Half-Assini already knew everything the textbooks would later "discover" about reef collapse.

The Tide Line is the curriculum she now teaches in schools along the same coast — and a quiet argument that conservation has been on the syllabus the whole time, just in a different language.
