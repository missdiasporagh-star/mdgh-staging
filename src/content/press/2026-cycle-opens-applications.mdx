The 2026 cycle of Miss Diaspora Ghana opens applications today, with three changes from prior years:

- **Broader eligibility.** Women of Ghanaian descent based in any country are eligible. Past cycles required Ghana-side residency. The platform's diaspora mandate is now reflected in its application rules.
- **More host cities.** Crown Camp will run in two cities this cycle — Accra and London — with a hybrid programming model bridging both cohorts.
- **Deeper alumni track.** Alumni queens from prior cycles join Crown Camp as mentors AND as program leads — running curriculum modules in their areas of expertise.

Application closes at the end of June. Selection runs through August. Crown Camp begins in September. The finale is held at the National Theatre, Accra, in mid-November.
