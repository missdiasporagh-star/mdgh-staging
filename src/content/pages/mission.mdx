## How the pillars work together

Heritage without leadership is performance. Leadership without heritage is rootless. Impact without either is short-lived. The platform is shaped to grow all three at once — through the cycle, through the alumni network, and through the public moment of the crown.

## What we don't do

Pageantry as spectacle. Beauty as the only measure. Reign as a year of photo-ops. The platform's contract with each queen is that she leaves changed and changing her world — measured not by her sash, but by what the next twelve months look like. (Edit with real anti-mission language.)
