## Why partner with the platform

The Miss Diaspora Ghana audience reaches across thirty cities, from Accra to Hamburg to Toronto. They are women in their twenties and thirties — high-earning, culturally engaged, hard to reach through traditional advertising. (Edit with real audience numbers when finalised.)

## What partnership looks like in practice

Partnership is not logo-on-a-banner. Each partner has a named program touchpoint — a cycle they sponsor, a scholarship they fund, a city they host. The brand walks with the platform for the year, and the integrations are built around what the brand actually wants to be known for. ## What we don't do

We don't do paid posts, branded sashes, or transactional crown placement. The platform's value to its audience is its editorial integrity — and that is the asset you are buying when you become a partner.
