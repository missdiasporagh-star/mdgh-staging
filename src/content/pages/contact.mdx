## What to send where

**Press** — pitches, interview requests, embargoed announcements: email or DM Instagram. Editorial team responds within forty-eight hours on weekdays.

**Partnerships** — sponsorship inquiries, brand collaborations, in-kind support: email is best. See [the sponsors page](/sponsors) for tier details first.

**Contestants** — application questions, eligibility checks, document submissions: WhatsApp is fastest during application windows. See [the apply page](/apply) for the full application process.

**Alumni** — current and past queens looking to connect into the network: email and we will introduce you to the alumni coordinator.

## Office

The platform is registered as Nubian Crown Company Limited in Accra, Ghana. Mail-in inquiries can be sent care of the partnerships team via email — we will share a postal address case-by-case.
